# Sarvottam Cables Ltd (SRVCABLE) — Research Brief

## Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables Ltd is a manufacturer of power cables, wires, and conductors [S3]. In Q1 FY27, the company reported consolidated revenue of ₹1,248 crore (up 18.0% YoY) and net profit of ₹82 crore (up 5.9% YoY) [S3], supported by strong demand from power transmission and data-centre projects [S3]. The order book stands healthy at ₹3,900 crore [S3], while the company manages a comfortable leverage position with a net debt-to-equity ratio of 0.35 times [S2]. 

---

## Bull case
* **Robust Order Book and Demand Runway:** The company's order book increased to ₹3,900 crore as of June 30, 2026, up 23.8% from ₹3,150 crore a year prior [S3]. High-margin segments like data-centre cables currently contribute 9% of domestic revenue, with management targeting to double this share within two years [S2].
* **Capacity Expansion on Track:** The company is setting up a new plant in Bharuch, Gujarat, with a total capital expenditure of ₹620 crore (approximately 70% of which has already been spent) [S2]. This plant will expand total cable capacity by roughly 30% and is on schedule to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27 [S3].
* **Increasing Global and Institutional Footprint:** Export contributions rose to 21% of revenue in Q1 FY27 compared to 16% in Q1 FY26 [S3]. Simultaneously, institutional investor confidence is rising, with Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs) increasing their shareholding from 9.2% to 11.8%, and Domestic Institutional Investors (DIIs) increasing theirs from 12.7% to 14.6% over the last year [S8].
* **Substantial Reduction in Promoter Pledging:** Although promoter pledging historically raised governance concerns when it peaked at 35% in March 2024 [S4], official exchange filings show that pledged shares have steadily declined to 4.1% of the promoter group's holdings as of June 2026 [S8].

---

## Bear case
* **Inability to Protect Near-Term Margins:** EBITDA margins contracted by 170 basis points YoY to 11.2% in Q1 FY27, hit by a 14% quarterly increase in copper prices [S1, S3]. While two-thirds of contracts contain price-variation clauses, they reset with a lag of one to two quarters, exposing the company to near-term margin pressure during raw material spikes [S2].
* **Stretching Working Capital Cycle:** Receivable days have increased significantly from 78 days to 96 days over the past year [S2]. This deterioration is primarily driven by delayed collections from two large state utility customers [S2].
* **Substantial Regulatory Dispute:** The company has received a GST demand order from the Joint Commissioner, CGST, Mumbai, for ₹46.3 crore (inclusive of interest and penalty) regarding input tax credit claims from FY2020-21 and FY2021-22 [S7]. While the company intends to appeal, this dispute represents a material contingent liability [S7].

---

## Open questions
* **Unreconciled Revenue Discrepancy:** There is a major conflict in reporting for Q1 FY27. While official company exchange filings state that revenue from operations was **₹1,248 crore** (up 18% YoY) [S3], mainstream financial media reported a figure of **₹1,428 crore** (up 35% YoY) [S1], a claim repeated by stock advisory blogs [S5]. Investors should rely on the official ₹1,248 crore exchange filing [S3].
* **Entity-Mismatch Clutter:** Local news reported that a "Sarvottam Cable Network" was fined ₹8 lakh for illegal overhead wiring in Nagpur [S6]. Retail investors should note that this refers to an unrelated local cable TV/broadband company operated by Mr. Suresh Patil [S6] and has no relation to Sarvottam Cables Ltd (NSE: SRVCABLE).
* **Unregistered Promoter Hype vs. Official Guidance:** Self-proclaimed market blogs are heavily promoting the stock as a "strong buy" with speculative price targets of ₹1,450 (60% upside) based on incorrect 35% revenue growth figures [S5]. Retail investors must weigh this hype against the management's actual, conservative FY27 revenue growth guidance of 15% to 17% [S2].
